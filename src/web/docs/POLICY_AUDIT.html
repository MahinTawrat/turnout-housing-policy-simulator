D is ACS detached stock, B the five-year permit reference, V permit valuation per unit with a documented state fallback, and R ties-to-even rounding. Stock-based additions and permit-based responses have different outcome definitions.

Settings are assumptions, not confidence intervals. Cost ratios are capped at 30% before response. Fee savings equal a 50% cut to standardized $50,000 fees. Parking cost assumptions and permit valuations lack a common-dollar adjustment. No household-growth, rent, crowding or affordability measure enters these production equations.

Cost-policy state reconciliation proportionally allocates incomplete county permit coverage, especially material in New Mexico. It does not locate the unassigned projects. State transit uses direct exposure, so it need not equal summed counties.
