Validation and reproducibility

Implementation tests check whether code follows its stated rules. Historical tests assess limited predictive behavior. Neither establishes causal policy effects, site feasibility or future construction.

Permit-baseline evaluation

Four candidates are compared on declared development folds. Calibration and the 2021–2025 final holdout are separate. Latest-year flat continuation was selected. Mean state holdout error is about 12.0%; Minnesota's is about 6.4%. These summarize different scopes. Refreshed stress ranges have no independent coverage test. History is a revised vintage, not a real-time experiment.

Download development scores, final holdout predictions, calibration predictions and deployment error history. County references are state allocations, with no separate county accuracy claim.

Population and household evidence

Short population pseudo holdouts use revised Vintage 2025 observations. Latest-year trend has lower population-weighted error than the smoothed component estimator in both tests. The central three-year continuation is a transparent smoothing scenario, not a proven best forecast.

160 published California/NC projection comparisons are available. Agreement between forecasts is not observed future accuracy and does not validate national household formation. Download independent comparisons and population/household scenarios.

Missing observations and reconciliation

County permits sum to 1,428,446; official unorganized/balance records explain the 3,170-unit gap to 1,431,616. Cost-policy state reconciliation proportionally allocates coverage differences, especially material in New Mexico. State transit need not equal county sums.

Fourteen Census-withheld Suffolk County tracts propagate unavailable transit results to the county, state and U.S. A published county housing estimate minus available tract sum is a coverage gap, not a separately measured missing-tract count or statistical interval. The manifest lists exact tracts, notice and hashes.

What checks do not establish

They do not validate take-up, local zoning, pro formas, completion timing, future rents, a shortage closed, joint policy effects or a five-year local household forecast. Representative display checks are not an exhaustive browser matrix or screen-reader certification. See limits.
