What the tool cannot establish

The tool compares assumptions and identifies useful local checks. Reproducible calculations do not certify delivery or the best community policy.

LimitMeaning
Local legal eligibilityCheck adopted rules, fee schedules, overlays, effective dates and newly eligible parcels. Stock and exposure are proxies.
Response assumptionsParticipation, redevelopment, uplift and cost response are conditional sensitivity inputs; local causal responses are not calibrated.
Sites and financeLand, infrastructure, rents, complete costs, financing and returns are unverified.
Completions and timingPermits require separate cancellation, construction-lag, inspection and occupancy evidence.
Household forecastsShort revised-vintage tests do not validate five-year household formation. Size and group-quarter share remain constant.
New-building needLosses, reserves, usable vacancies, completions, conversions and independent suppressed formation are missing.
Affordability and mixCurrent problems do not establish future rents, bedrooms, tenure or income restrictions.
Transit measurementUniform tract housing, straight-distance buffers and projection/datum approximations limit interpretation. Missing weights remain missing.
Policy combinationsThe reforms can affect the same projects and cannot be added together.
Historical geographyCounty changes and unallocated permits limit local comparisons and proportional state cost reconciliation.

Large percentages can reflect small permit references. Declining household continuation does not eliminate repair, affordability, accessibility or replacement needs. Overlapping housing problems are not summed into missing homes.

The current official transit/ACS pipeline is reproducible. Its predecessor's incomplete extraction trail is preserved as a frozen reference, not evidence of exact recovery. Withheld source observations leave Suffolk County, New York and U.S. transit point results unavailable. See methods, validation and development checks.
