Local checks before acting

Use results to start a local investigation. No site or project is certified feasible.

ReformNext check
ADUCurrent rules, eligible sites, utilities, owner interest, design/cost and likely rents.
Missing middleSingle-family-only exposure, existing buildings, redevelopment economics, family layouts and infrastructure.
FeesActual fee schedule and stalled projects; infrastructure-funding consequences.
TransitNewly allowed density, walking access, sites, service, costs and intended affordability.
ParkingBinding minimums, market/financing requirements, voluntary parking and actual spaces/costs avoided.

A full assessment needs dated land, hard/soft costs, financing, rents, vacancy, timing and returns. Permit valuation omits parts of development cost. Existing crowding/cost burden does not establish economics or future affordable rents. Public local resources can support follow-up; they are not an ingested national parcel model.
